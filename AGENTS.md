# Agent notes

## Graphical aesthetic

The game looks minimal and graphic. Unless otherwise specified (e.g. the user asks for multiple
variations in various graphical styles), everything drawn should follow this:

- **Matte, flat colours.** No gradients, glows, gloss, soft shadows or shading bands. A surface is one
  colour.
- **Hard contrasts.** Shapes read by silhouette against what is behind them (a pale vehicle on dark
  ground under a black sky), not by outlines, highlights or detail.
- **One colour per thing.** The terrain is one colour, a vehicle is one colour. Don't colour-code the
  parts of an object.
- **Rudimentary shapes.** Build things from a few plain primitives: slabs, discs, lines, triangles.
  Show how something works or moves with a cut-out or a gap rather than with added decoration (a wheel
  is a disc with triangles cut out of it, so that it can be seen to turn).
- **Nothing childish-looking.** No cartoon proportions, rounded "toy" forms, cute faces, bright candy
  colours or decorative doodads. When in doubt, leave it out.
- **Keep the palette small and muted**: black, off-white, and a few desaturated colours per scene.
